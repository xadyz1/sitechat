FAQ

Common questions

How does the trial work?

You can test SwiftChat without commitment. Set up your knowledge base, test the AI response quality on your site, and select a plan when you are ready to launch live.

Do I need developer skills to set this up?

No technical expertise is required. If you can copy and paste a single HTML line into your web builder (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, React, HTML), you can deploy SwiftChat in minutes.

How does SwiftChat keep information accurate?

SwiftChat relies strictly on the content you provide via web crawling, PDF uploads, and direct Q&A overrides. Every response includes direct citations back to your source pages.

Can I remove the SwiftChat brand badge?

Yes. The Advanced Plan includes complete white-labeling, allowing you to hide all SwiftChat branding and present a fully customized experience to your users.

Can I manage multiple websites?

Yes, you can create separate widgets, isolated knowledge bases, and dedicated analytics for multiple websites directly from your central account dashboard.
